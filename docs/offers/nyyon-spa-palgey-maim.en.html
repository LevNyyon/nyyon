10. Dispute Resolution

If a dispute arises, the complaining Party must provide written notice describing the issue.

The Parties will attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith within fourteen (14) days.

If the dispute cannot be resolved, it shall be submitted to binding arbitration in Tel Aviv, Israel, in accordance with the rules of the Israel Institute of Commercial Arbitration (IICA).

11. Governing Law

The courts of Tel Aviv, Israel shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any legal matters arising from this Agreement.

12. Entire Agreement

This Agreement represents the entire understanding between the Parties regarding the Services.

ANNEX A

Annex A: Scope of Work

Pilot Engagement (Proof of Concept) · AI Implementation for Palgey Maim

A1. Background and Objective

Palgey Maim is an engineering-design firm. The objective of this engagement is to identify a single workflow in which AI implementation yields a measurable time saving, and to deliver a working tool focused on that workflow, within a predefined timeframe and budget.

The engagement is structured as a Proof of Concept (POC): a short, measurable commitment that allows the Client to evaluate the business value and Nyyon's way of working before expanding the engagement.

A2. Approach

The engagement is delivered in four phases:

Phase 1. Champion Appointment

At the outset, the Client appoints a single senior point of contact (the "Champion") for the engagement: the person Nyyon works with throughout the process, who participates in key decisions and receives the deliverable and training at handoff. A single point of contact ensures fast decision-making, knowledge continuity, and successful adoption of the tool at the end of the engagement.

Phase 2. Discovery (one business day, on-site)

Nyyon conducts a discovery workshop with the Champion and relevant stakeholders, maps the candidate workflows, and defines together with the Client the single highest-value project and its scope.

Phase 3. Development (two weeks)

Nyyon develops the tool on the Client's infrastructure, with regular check-ins with the Champion for status updates, progress review, and required adjustments throughout the period.

During the second week, an additional on-site day takes place at the Client's premises, with the tool installed and running on-prem, for hands-on experience and review by the Champion ahead of the final delivery.

Phase 4. Delivery and Handoff

Delivery of a working tool operating in the Client's environment to the Champion, accompanied by operational documentation and a handoff and training session. Work product transfers to the Client in accordance with Section 3 of this Agreement.

A3. Deliverables

  • Solution Spec document (at the end of the Discovery phase)
  • One working tool, deployed and operational in the Client's environment
  • Operational documentation: operation, running, and maintenance
  • Handoff and training session for the Champion and the relevant team

A4. Acceptance Criteria

The tool is deemed complete upon meeting the criteria defined in the Solution Spec at the end of the Discovery phase, and a successful demonstration of its operation in the Client's environment.

A5. Timeline

  • Champion appointment: at the Effective Date
  • Discovery: one business day, scheduled
  • Development: up to 10 business days from completion of Discovery
  • On-site review day: during the second week of Development, with the tool running on-prem
  • Delivery: upon completion of the Development phase

A6. Out of Scope

The engagement is focused on a single tool. It does not include: development of additional systems or tools, integrations not defined during Discovery, large-scale data migration, ongoing support or a service level agreement (SLA) after handoff, and third-party software licensing (see A8). Extensions are handled as scope additions in accordance with Section 6 of this Agreement.

A7. Client Responsibilities and Assumptions

  • Provision of access to systems, data, and stakeholders required to perform the Services
  • Appointment of a Champion and their availability as the point of contact throughout the engagement
  • Opening third-party service accounts (Anthropic, Cloudflare) in the Client's name, or authorization to set them up
  • Timely decisions and feedback, to meet the timeline

A8. Third-Party Licensing and Infrastructure

Licensing and infrastructure components are billed at cost, directly to the Client and in the Client's name, with no markup by Nyyon. Initial estimate, to be refined at the end of the Discovery phase once usage volume is defined:

  • Claude (Anthropic): approximately $100 one-time (development phase); approximately $20 to $200 per month ongoing, depending on usage volume.
  • Cloudflare: no base cost; approximately $5 to $25 per month.

A9. Fees and Payment

  • Pilot (Proof of Concept) fee: NIS 9,000 plus VAT.
  • Includes: discovery, development, delivery, and training.
  • Payment terms: 50% upon commencement, 50% upon delivery (in accordance with Section 5 of this Agreement).
  • Third-party licensing and infrastructure costs (A8) are separate and billed at cost.

A10. Path to Ongoing Engagement

Upon completion of the pilot, and subject to its results, Nyyon offers an ongoing AI implementation engagement across the company: development of additional tools, advisory, and training, at a scope and rate to be defined in a dedicated annex. The Proof of Concept serves as a measurable basis for defining the continuation.
