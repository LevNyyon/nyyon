## The idea

You wanted someone part-time inside the company building AI. This is the same
thing, but packaged as one fixed first project so you see a real working result
before any ongoing commitment. One person, in your office, on your accounts.
Not an agency.

## What you get

1. **Discovery day, on-site, 1 day.** I sit with your team in Yokneam, map
   where AI actually saves real hours, and we pick ONE project worth building.
   You leave the day with a one-page spec: what it does, what it needs, what
   "done" looks like.

2. **Build sprint, 2 weeks.** I build that one tool. Working, on your stack,
   with short check-ins along the way so there are no surprises.

3. **Handoff.** End of week 2 you have 1 working tool running on your own
   accounts, plus a short plain-language doc on how it works and how to run it.
   No lock-in, nothing hidden.

## Timeline

| When | What |
|------|------|

| Week 1 to 2 | Build plus check-ins |

## What it costs

**Engagement fee (fixed): 9,000 NIS.**
Covers the discovery day, the 2-week build, and handoff. Priced as a test
project, half the standard pilot rate, so the first step is low-risk for you.
One price, no hourly surprises.

**Tool costs (run on your own accounts, you own them):**

| Item | What it is | Build phase | Ongoing |
|------|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Claude (Anthropic) | The AI engine the tool and I use | up to ~$100 one time | ~$20 to $200 / mo, depends on usage, tuned during build |
| Infra (Cloudflare or hosting) | Where the tool runs | free tier | ~$5 to $25 / mo |

These sit on **your** Anthropic and Cloudflare accounts and are billed to you
directly. Nothing marked up, nothing you cannot see. Typical small internal
tool lands near the low end. We size the real number together on the discovery
day once we know the project.

## After the pilot (optional)

If it works, we move to the part-time setup you wanted: roughly 1 day a week
on-site, building the next tools. We set that rate after the pilot has proven
the value, not before.

## Why this shape

You asked for an implementer, not an agency. This keeps it exactly that: one
person, your office, your accounts, one working thing in two weeks. The pilot
is the trial run for the part-time relationship, with a concrete deliverable
either way.
