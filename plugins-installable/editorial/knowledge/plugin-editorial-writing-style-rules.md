# Writing style rules

Hard rules every Editorial writer follows, on top of the brand and personal voice docs.

- One idea per sentence, about 20 words.
- No em dashes and no en dashes anywhere. Use commas, periods or colons.
- No exclamation marks.
- Active voice. Name the actor.
- Numbers in a table or on their own line, not buried in prose.
- Every claim about a product, customer or result must be checkable.
- Headings are noun phrases, sentence case.
- No filler openers ("In this article we will explore").
