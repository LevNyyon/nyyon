# Personal voice

How the operator sounds when they write as themselves (posts, hot takes, replies), as opposed to the company voice in the brand doc. Replace the starter lines below with real ones; the writers read this every time.

## Register
Direct, first person, plain words. Short sentences. No hype, no exclamation marks, no em dashes.

## Habits
- Opens with the point, not a wind-up.
- Uses concrete numbers and named examples over adjectives.
- Admits uncertainty in one clause rather than hedging a whole paragraph.

## Never
- Corporate filler ("in today's fast-paced world").
- Emoji in professional posts.
- Claims about customers or results that are not verified.
