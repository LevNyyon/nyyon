# News search — when and how

One tool: `search_news(query, limit?)`. Keyless Google News search; results are
headlines with source, date and link.

Use it when the operator asks what is happening around a topic, a company, a
person, or their industry — anything where fresh outside news answers better
than memory. Summarize the headlines conversationally; include the link when
the operator will want to read on.

The Digest's **search channel** uses the same provider on its own: it reads the
topics listed in `plugin-digest-search-topics` and files fresh headlines into
the morning brief. To change what the digest watches, edit that doc — not this
one.
